# Notes

Chronological working notes for Gild.

Name files `YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md`, for example:

- `2026-11-02-kickoff.md`
- `2026-11-04-dispatch-model.md`
- `2026-11-08-pricing-experiments.md`

## What to write

- Observations from research or user conversations.
- Constraints you discovered.
- Options you are comparing.
- Questions you cannot answer yet.
- Links to spikes, diagrams, screenshots, and external references.

## What not to write

- Decisions with no context. Put consequential decisions in [`../decisions/`](../decisions/README.md).
- Meeting transcripts with no summary. Keep the conclusion and the open questions.
- A plan that pretends uncertainty does not exist.

## Promote to a decision when

- the choice affects more than one module or team;
- the choice is expensive to reverse;
- someone will ask “why did we do it this way?” later;
- the choice depends on a constraint that might change.

Use the [note template](_template.md) if it helps. Delete the sections you do not need.
