# Where DDD came from

Eric Evans developed DDD from consulting work on large enterprise systems from the early 1990s to the 2003 book. His projects were large Java and Smalltalk systems in finance, shipping, insurance, and manufacturing automation. The cargo-shipping example in the book is loosely based on a real container-shipping project.

DDD was a synthesis of that consulting experience: many projects, many approaches, and many outcomes. The problems were large, complex systems with domain experts, multiple teams, legacy integration, and rules that had to live for years.

The book presents the modeling and design techniques that successful teams used to align complex software with business needs and keep projects agile as systems grew.
